Jewmaica is dedicated to preserving, expressing, and sharing the values and traditions of Jewish life in Jamaica, with a particular appreciation for the Sephardic tradition and its enduring connection to the island.

OUR STORY

The Story of Jewmaica

Jewmaica was founded by Michael A. Nunes through study and learning with Rabbi Haim Ovadia of Torah Ve’Ahava and minyan leadership in tefillah at SephardicU.

Jewmaica is dedicated to promoting the traditional values of Portugese (Sephardic) Judaism, extending the olive branch of the Torah verse, Vayikra 19:18,

וְאָהַבְתָּ לְרֵעֲךָ כָּמוֹךָ
“Love your neighbor as yourself.”
— Vayikra (Leviticus) 19:18

Through a deep commitment to Torah observance, Jewmaica seeks to express these values in our actions, homes, and communal engagement.

It is also focused on outreach to the Jamaican community, fostering loving understanding while upholding strong Torah values, as well as supporting the State of Israel and promoting safety and understanding for Jews everywhere.
