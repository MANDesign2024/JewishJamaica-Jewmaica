Welcome to Jewmaica

Jewmaica is a growing digital home for Torah learning, Jewish history, Portuguese (Sephardi) heritage, Jamaican Jewish genealogy, community information, and educational resources.

Our aim is to preserve, share, and develop the Jewish story of Jamaica while creating a place for Torah, learning, community, and connection.

ABOUT JEWMAICA

Our Story

Jewmaica is dedicated to exploring and preserving the Jewish experience in Jamaica while encouraging Torah learning, Jewish connection, and community.

The project brings together Torah teachings, Jewish history, Portuguese (Sephardi) heritage, genealogy, educational resources, media, and community information.

This website is intended to grow into a living digital archive and educational resource for Jewish Jamaica and for anyone interested in Jamaica's Jewish heritage.
